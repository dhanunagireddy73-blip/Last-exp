hackathon is an intensive, time-limited event where programmers, designers, and other professionals collaborate to solve a specific problem or develop a project, often a software prototype or application, within a compressed timeframe. Derived from "hacker" and "marathon," hackathons foster rapid innovation and skill development, allowing participants to network, learn, and showcase their abilities in a supportive and competitive environment.
